Mini-Homeworks

  • Mini-homeworks are worth 15% of the course grade.
  • Read the papers being presented before class.
  • Mini-homeworks will ask a few short questions:
    • What did you find interesting about the papers for this class?
    • What questions do you have about the papers for this class?
    • What do you hope to learn from the discussion?
  • Responses will be posted for everyone in the class to see.
  • Please write the mini-homeworks yourself. We want your thoughts, not responses generated by an LLM.

Presentation Tips

  • Practice the talk at least once and get feedback from a friend or classmate.
  • Focus on one or two key ideas or main results instead of trying to cover everything in the paper.
  • Use the minimum amount of notation needed. Present the cleanest, simplest setting and avoid unnecessary details and jargon.
  • It is fine to skim some parts of the paper and read the main parts carefully. For a long paper, reading about 20–30 pages can be enough to identify and understand the key ideas.
  • Make the main takeaways clear and connect the presentation to material covered in class where possible.
